Usernames: 1–32 letters, digits and . _ @ -; passwords 8–128 characters. A new user can log in at once; a removed user, or one whose password was changed, is asked to log in again at the next page load. Passwords are stored only as hashes and never shown. The users are kept in webusers.txt next to the maintenance login file (outside the database). The built-in user is set in config.py (WEB_USER / WEB_PASS) and cannot be changed here; the maintenance user can also open the web pages, and changing its password here also changes the Maintenance login.

Autopurge
days
When enabled, flight & meteo records older than the threshold are automatically purged once per day. GPS Quality, Approach History and the GPS Episode Log are never auto-purged.
Archive before purge: every flight & meteo purge (automatic and manual) first copies the observations and their flights to a monthly archive file (modes_meteo_archive_YYYY-MM.db, see below), and the daily retention purges of approach_series and departure_profiles do the same. Nothing is deleted unless the copy succeeded and was checked.
Archive Files & Snapshots
Folder: —

Monthly archive files hold the purged observations (all columns, same ids) with their flights, and the archived approach series and departure profiles. A snapshot is a compact copy of the whole running database (all tables) at that moment, e.g. for analysis on another computer. Both are ordinary SQLite files; tools/archive_query.py combines the archive files with the running database.
