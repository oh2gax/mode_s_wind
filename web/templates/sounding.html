Time–height section · hourly area profiles
Each column is one UTC hour of the hourly profile archive (all aircraft within the sounding radius; the hour in progress is added live), each row an altitude layer (1 000 ft up to 10 000 ft, 2 000 ft above); the height scale is stretched near the ground (ISA pressure on the right). Temperature anomaly: the layer's temperature minus its mean over the shown period — warm (red) and cold (blue) air masses; a front or trough passes as a sloping boundary, the night inversion as a cold layer near the ground. Temperature change 3 h: warming / cooling as it happens (advection). Wind speed: jets and low-level jets. Lapse rate: between adjacent layers — inversions violet / blue, steep or dry-unstable orange / red. Vertical motion (FL050–FL200): rising (green) or sinking (orange) air in cm/s, from the part of the temperature change that the wind does not bring — sinking air warms, rising air cools (adiabatic method; about ±1 cm/s of noise, cloud heating not included, the newest hours less certain). Vorticity (about FL130–FL390): the spin of the wind field over 200 km from a plane fit of the aircraft winds — cyclonic (purple) in troughs and on the cold side of a jet, anticyclonic (gold) in ridges and highs (about ±2 ×10⁻⁵ s⁻¹ of noise; the long-term mean is probably 2–3 units too anticyclonic). Faint cells rest on fewer than 3 replies; night hours often have only a few aircraft (red bars in the aircraft strip). Hover for values; click a column to open that hour on the Skew-T.

Verified launches (area profile ±1 h around the launch)
Each stored Jokioinen sounding is compared with the Mode S area profile of ±1 h around its launch (all aircraft within the sounding radius; layers with ≥ 3 replies within the sounding's height range; sonde values interpolated in log pressure). Every archived hourly profile is also compared with the newest sounding launched by then: the difference grows as the sounding ages and the air mass changes — the reason for a live profile. Jokioinen is about 100 km north-west and inland (cooler nights near the ground). Winds before 10 Oct 2026 are without the per-airframe heading correction.

Temperature is computed from each aircraft's true airspeed (BDS 5,0) and Mach number (BDS 6,0): T = 288.15 K · (TAS / (661.47 kt · M))². One reply scatters about ±1.3 K at cruise and ±3–4 K on approach, so each level shows the mean of all replies in the layer (10 % trimmed) (1 000 ft layers below 10 000 ft, 2 000 ft above). Pressure is the standard-atmosphere pressure of the pressure altitude.
