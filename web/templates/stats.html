Time between successive replies of the same aircraft as heard here. Final: below 5 000 ft within 25 NM of EFHK; terminal: below 10 000 ft within 60 NM; en route: the rest. Replies closer than 0.5 s (ADS-B squitters: 0.15 s) are counted as near-duplicates (the same register answered twice, e.g. to two interrogators), gaps over 60 s are not intervals. The receiver hears the aircraft's replies (1090 MHz), not the interrogations (1030 MHz): a longer interval can also be a reply that did not reach Tuusula.
